# Reaper

REAPER is my main go-to DAW since 2016. 
Over the time, I have created a lot of presets, tracktemplates and more, that I would like to share.
I only share plugins that are valuable for others and that are 100% compatible stock effects.

[Back to Home](/README.md)

### How to install
Go to your REAPER folder and you can drag and drop the folders into the REAPER folder.
To not mix it up with other effects, all presets and templates are into the fortress folder.
